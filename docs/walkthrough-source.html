At a glance

A website that presents Brain Industries as the specialists you are

The new site replaces the Wix website with a faster, independent and far more informative experience — built to earn the trust of engineers, plant managers and procurement teams in hazardous-area industries.

Positioned as experts

25+ years’ experience, a qualified M.I.E and the full Ex lifecycle — classification, supply, installation, audits and certification — are front and centre.

Interactive, not just words

A honeycomb service selector with custom symbols, an Ex quick-reference tool and a step-by-step InspectX walkthrough invite visitors to explore.

Industry-specific information

Where explosive atmospheres occur, zones and protection levels, and the SANS standards behind each service — demonstrating knowledge, not just claiming it.

InspectX given its own stage

A dedicated page and homepage feature for your QR-code-driven inspection system, with the app and manager portal illustrated.

Built for phones

Full-screen menu, swipeable service selector and a call / request-a-quote bar that follows visitors down the page.

Owned, fast and secure

No Wix fees or lock-in. Hosted on GitHub Pages, loads quickly, with POPIA consent, Privacy Notice and PAIA Manual linked throughout.

  • Office address — old site: 378 Main Street; profile & PAIA: 402 Julius Jeppe Street (currently used).
  • Registration number 2016/099333/07 (from the Privacy Notice).
  • M.I.E — Master Installation Electrician; any name/registration to show.
  • Products — current TEP Ex supply, the HALO LED range, and Roto Reflector colours.
  • Certificates of Compliance — registration of the person issuing CoCs.
  • Dust classification — that dust areas (Zones 20–22) are classified as well as gas.
  • Gallery captions and photo ownership; rights to the LED lighting image.
  • LinkedIn link — personal profile or a company page.
  • PAIA Manual — appears to contain another company’s content (solar/PV records); needs review by your Information Officer.
  • InspectX — SAP integration status; illustrative app/portal screens are acceptable.

Going live

  1. Enquiry email. Choose the address that should receive website enquiries; we create a free Web3Forms key for it.
  2. Approve the preview. Final content changes are made in one place and the site rebuilds automatically.
  3. Publish. The site is published on GitHub Pages (free hosting).
  4. Point the domain. Update the DNS for brainindustriessa.com (and www) — about 15 minutes of work; up to 24 hours to take effect.
  5. Check & switch off Wix. Once the new site is live on HTTPS, cancel the Wix plan. If the domain is registered through Wix, it is transferred first.

Email is unaffected

Your email addresses (@imagine.co.za) are separate from the website and will not change.
